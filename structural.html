About Structural Estimation

Costiq delivers accurate structural estimation services for contractors, builders, engineers, and developers. We prepare detailed quantity takeoffs and pricing reports for concrete, reinforcement, and steel structures.

Our estimating process follows CSI standards to ensure every structural component is measured accurately for bid preparation and project budgeting.

CSI Divisions We Cover

Division 03

Concrete estimation including slabs, footings, foundations, columns, beams, and reinforced concrete systems.

Division 05
