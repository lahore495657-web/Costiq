Our Mission

CostiQ Estimation exists to revolutionize the construction bidding process in the USA market. Our mission is to help contractors eliminate guesswork, reduce financial risk, and increase profitability through advanced estimation systems, real-time cost databases, and professional Trades-based breakdowns. We believe construction success starts from accurate numbers. A wrong estimate can destroy profit margins, delay projects, and damage client trust. That is why we built a system where every project is analyzed deeply before delivering final BOQs. Our team works across multiple departments including estimation, pricing, review, QA, and client support. Every project goes through strict quality checks to ensure accuracy and reliability.
