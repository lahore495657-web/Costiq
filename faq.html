Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our construction estimating services, delivery process, file formats, pricing structure, and CSI division coverage.

How do I submit my project drawings?
You can easily submit your project drawings through our contact form or email. We accept PDF, DWG, JPG, and PNG formats. Once you upload files, our estimating team reviews them carefully and starts preparing a detailed quantity takeoff and cost estimate. We ensure every submission is handled securely and professionally.
What type of projects do you handle?
We handle residential, commercial, and industrial construction projects across all CSI divisions. This includes architectural, structural, MEP (mechanical, electrical, plumbing), site work, renovations, and even solar projects. No matter the size of the project, we provide scalable estimating solutions.
How fast is your turnaround time?
Our standard turnaround time is 24–48 hours depending on project complexity. Smaller residential jobs are usually delivered faster, while large commercial or multi-trade projects may take slightly longer to ensure accuracy and completeness.
Do you follow CSI divisions in estimates?
Yes, all our estimates are strictly organized according to CSI divisions. This ensures clarity, consistency, and professional reporting standards that contractors and developers in the USA market expect for bidding and project execution.
Why should I outsource estimation work?
Outsourcing estimation saves you valuable time and reduces workload pressure. Instead of spending hours on calculations, you can focus on winning projects and managing execution. Our expert estimators ensure higher accuracy, better bid competitiveness, and improved profit margins.
Do you provide revisions if needed?
Yes, we offer revisions based on client feedback. If any clarification or adjustment is required, we refine the estimate to ensure complete satisfaction and alignment with project requirements.
