CSI Division 08 – Openings Detailed Takeoff

How Our Estimators Perform Openings Takeoff

Division 08 includes all doors, frames, windows, glazing, storefront systems, curtain walls, skylights, louvers, and hardware systems.

Our estimators carefully review architectural plans, elevations, schedules, door legends, window schedules, and specifications to prepare accurate quantity takeoffs.

  • Reading door schedules to identify door types, sizes, ratings, and hardware requirements.
  • Counting all single doors, double doors, overhead doors, access doors, and specialty openings.
  • Measuring window sizes directly from elevations and window schedules.
  • Calculating curtain wall square footage from building elevations.
  • Taking off glazing quantities including tempered, laminated, insulated, and fire-rated glass.
  • Quantifying aluminum storefront framing systems and associated accessories.
  • Reviewing hardware groups including hinges, closers, locks, panic bars, and handles.
  • Identifying specialty openings such as skylights, louvers, shutters, and smoke vents.

CSI Division 09 – Finishes Detailed Takeoff

Detailed Drywall & Finishes Estimation Process

CSI Division 09 is one of the most critical architectural estimation scopes and includes drywall systems, ceilings, flooring, painting, plaster, and decorative finishes.

  • Measuring drywall wall areas from floor plans and reflected ceiling plans.
  • Calculating gypsum board quantities based on wall height, layers, and board type.
  • Measuring metal stud framing linear footage.
  • Calculating ceiling grid systems and acoustic ceiling tiles.
  • Taking off flooring quantities including carpet, vinyl, epoxy, wood flooring, and ceramic tiles.
  • Measuring painting areas for walls, ceilings, doors, frames, and trims.
  • Identifying waterproofing and wall protection systems.
  • Calculating material wastage and production rates.
